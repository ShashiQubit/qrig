# qrig benchmark results

Qiskit 2.5.2, qrig 1.0.0 (backend `process`), Python 3.11.14, seed 20260925.

*Before*: Qiskit's preset level-3 pass manager, basis {cx, u}, `qubits_initially_zero=False` (circuits compiled as unitaries). *After*: the same pass manager with `QrigOptimize` as a pre-pass (`pre_init` stage). Values are means over the samples. Generated by `benchmarks/benchmark.py`.

## A. Workloads

| workload | why | samples | CX before | CX after | gates before | gates after | depth before | depth after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Random controlled Clifford+T (4q, 40 gates) | unstructured: little for qrig to find | 10 | 213.6 | 214.2 | 476.8 | 478.7 | 369.0 | 370.4 |
| Grover (Qiskit library), 5q, 2 iterations | no adjacent controlled gates qrig can merge | 1 | 144.0 | 144.0 | 318.0 | 318.0 | 257.0 | 257.0 |
| Multiplexer, 4 controls, repeated branches | equation (e) C0(f);C1(f) = id+f, and (a) | 5 | 601.6 | 287.8 | 1282.0 | 621.0 | 1019.6 | 468.4 |
| Repeated controlled phase C^3(T) x4 | equation (a) C(f);C(g) = C(f;g), T^4 = Z | 1 | 80.0 | 14.0 | 166.0 | 32.0 | 126.0 | 27.0 |
| Compute/uncompute U;U^-1 (4q, 30 gates) | stack-wise cancellation of controlled gates | 5 | 313.8 | 222.8 | 694.8 | 490.8 | 541.6 | 381.2 |
| C^3(H);C^3(V);C^3(V^-1);C^3(H) | equation (a) + one-qubit gate fusion | 1 | 64.0 | 0.0 | 143.0 | 0.0 | 115.0 | 0.0 |
